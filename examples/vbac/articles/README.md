# VBAC corpus — articles

PDFs are not included in this repository. The 13-paper corpus was supplied at the start of the run, not retrieved from a structured search (this trial began at the extraction step, not the setup step). The included papers are clinical studies, most of which are paywalled.

## Included papers (target filenames)

```
articles/
├── ashwal2015.pdf              — Ashwal E et al., Israel cohort
├── bhide2016.pdf               — Bhide A et al., London cohort
├── chaillet2013.pdf            — Chaillet N et al., Quebec cohort + Grobman calculator validation
├── clark2008_fetal_injury.pdf  — Clark SL et al., case report (off-topic; flagged in audit)
├── girma2021.pdf               — Girma A et al., Ethiopia cohort
├── gonen2004.pdf               — Gonen R et al., Israel cohort (Bishop-score predictors)
├── hollard2006.pdf             — Hollard AL et al., Southern California cohort
├── knight2013.pdf              — Knight HE et al., UK cohort
├── konheim-kalkstein2017.pdf   — Konheim-Kalkstein YL et al., psychological predictors of TOLAC attempt
├── lazarou2021.pdf             — Lazarou A et al., Germany cohort
├── obeidat2013.pdf             — Obeidat B et al., North Jordan cohort
├── olagbuji2010.pdf            — Olagbuji BN et al., Benin City Nigeria cohort
└── seffah2014.pdf              — Seffah JD et al., Ghana cohort (high uterine-rupture rate)
```

The audit flagged Clark 2008 (a case report about routine vacuum at elective repeat CS, not VBAC predictors) and Konheim-Kalkstein 2017 (outcome is `tolac_attempt`, not `vbac_success`) — both retained in the corpus but stratified during consolidation.
